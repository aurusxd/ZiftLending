Scope: single landing page `/` (Zift studio site). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: founders / product leads evaluating an AI build partner. Action: book a discovery call. Proof on hand: hero video only; portfolio is labeled synthetic until real work arrives. Constraints: tech.md is the pinned brief (sections, copy, palette, section badges, stack).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The strike is the hero. One real lightning take lands behind the headline and the page answers with light, instead of the category's gradient-blob hero with floating cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night ground #0a0a0a; one ember accent #FF6A2E→#FFB454 used only as light (strike flash, glow on hover, active underline, primary CTA fill); warm off-white #F4F1EC type; Archivo at 125% width for caps display, normal width for text; pill controls, hairline rules at 10% white.

STORY: A founder reads what Zift is in one line, watches it strike, sees how the studio works and what it built, and books a call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed video, bolt center; two-line expanded caps headline centered at up to 6rem; one studio sentence; white "See Our Work" and outline "Book Discovery Call" beneath; fixed header: logo, centered nav, accent "Book a call". Signature: the headline catches the video's 3.4s/3.7s flashes.

FORM: brief-pinned (tech.md); concept roll not run; code-led (no image generation).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
